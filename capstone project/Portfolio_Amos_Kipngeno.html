About Me

My name is Amos Kipngeno, born on June 24, 2001 in the picturesque town of Molo, Nakuru County. As the fifth-born in my family, my early years were shaped by the warm embrace of my parents and a close-knit community that placed education and hard work at the heart of everything.

From Kogo Junior Primary School — where I excelled in Science and Kiswahili, served as class captain, joined the choir, debating team, and environmental club — to Molo Academy Boys High School where I pursued the science stream and earned a Certificate of Merit in KCSE 2021, every chapter has been defined by perseverance and growth.

Now in my final year at Dedan Kimathi University of Technology, I have developed real-world digital solutions including an Electronic Queue Management System that cut medical centre wait times by 60%. My skills span the full web stack — HTML, CSS, JavaScript, PHP, Node.js, MySQL — and I am passionate about using technology to solve African problems and build a sustainable digital future for Kenya.

Climate Change & Environmental Crisis in Africa

Africa faces record droughts, catastrophic floods, and devastating heatwaves — all intensified by climate change. This essay documents the human cost across Malawi, Zimbabwe, Nigeria, Tanzania, and Kenya: parched farmland, displaced families, vanishing water sources, locust invasions, and communities struggling to survive.

Drought & Water Scarcity
Parched farmland in Malawi, Zambia and Zimbabwe where crop harvests dropped 40–50%. Families queue at scarce, contaminated wells as water sources dry to muddy pools.
🌊
Flooding & Displacement
Catastrophic floods in Nigeria (2022, 2025) killed hundreds and displaced over 1.3 million people. Children and elderly wade through floodwaters; families live in makeshift shelters.
🌱
Land Degradation
Severe land degradation in rural Tanzania forces farmers into poverty. Environmental shocks, lack of infrastructure, and conflict-driven disruptions destroy agricultural livelihoods.
🦗
Desert Locust Invasions
Climate-intensified locust swarms destroy entire crop seasons, threatening food security for 25 million people across Kenya, Ethiopia, and Somalia.
👨‍🌾
Impact on Rural Communities
Rural farmers in Mathari face economic devastation as climate shocks, lack of credit access, and supply chain disruption combine to destroy incomes and food security.
💡
Call to Action
Addressing these crises requires integrated water management, sustainable land-use practices, climate adaptation strategies, and investment in African-led environmental solutions.
